While Amazon leans heavily on recent sales velocity and keyword bidding, Cassini prioritizes listing completeness (100% Item Specifics), account trust metrics (Top Rated status, return policy, response time), and historical sell-through rates. Esolv optimizes both sides of the equation to ensure your listings receive maximum organic exposure.

Promoted Listings Standard operates on a Cost-Per-Sale (CPS) model where you choose an ad rate percentage and only pay when an item sells within 30 days of a click. Promoted Listings Advanced is Cost-Per-Click (CPC) keyword bidding that places your listing in the top 1-4 sponsored slots. We run an integrated blend of both to capture high-intent searchers while guarding profit margins.
