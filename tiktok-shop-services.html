Creators request or receive free product samples from your shop. They post shoppable videos featuring your product linked directly via the yellow/orange TikTok shopping cart. When viewers purchase, TikTok automatically deducts your pre-set affiliate commission (typically 10% to 20%) and pays the creator, with zero upfront ad cost to you.

Spark Ads allow brands to boost an existing organic post published from a creator’s personal TikTok account rather than the brand's own account. Viewers perceive it as genuine organic user-generated content (UGC), resulting in significantly higher watch times, lower click costs, and up to 3x higher conversion rates.

FBT is TikTok’s direct equivalent to Amazon FBA. You send inventory to TikTok’s authorized fulfillment centers in the US or UK. When an order is placed, TikTok picks, packs, and delivers it automatically. Using FBT eliminates late-dispatch violation risks and unlocks platform shipping subsidies.
