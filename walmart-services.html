Frequently Asked Questions

Key insights on scaling profitably on Walmart Marketplace.

Walmart curates its seller base carefully. You need a registered business entity (US EIN or foreign business equivalent), a proven track record of e-commerce sales (e.g. on Amazon or Shopify), verifiable B2C product reviews, and standard GTIN/UPC barcodes. Esolv manages the application process to ensure first-time approval.

WFS offers simple, transparent fee structures with zero dimensional weight upcharges for standard items, often saving sellers 15% to 25% compared to Amazon FBA fulfillment costs. WFS products automatically receive the 2-Day Shipping tag, significantly boosting Buy Box ownership and search rank.

Walmart enforces strict price parity rules. If their crawlers find the identical product listed for a lower price on Amazon, eBay, or your DTC website, they will automatically suppress your listing from search results. Esolv sets up automated cross-channel repricing to keep your products active and profitable.

Walmart Connect uses a first-price auction model for keyword search placement. Ads appear directly inside the main search grid, product carousels, and the Buy Box area. Because competition is lower on Walmart than Amazon, typical CPCs are 30% to 50% cheaper, producing exceptional ROAS when managed with precise negative keyword targeting.

You must maintain: On-time delivery rate ≥ 95%, seller-canceled rate ≤ 1.5%, minimum 250 completed orders in the last 90 days, 100% of product catalog achieving an LQS of 70%+, and strict adherence to marketplace trust standards. We manage your operations daily to guarantee badge acquisition and retention.
